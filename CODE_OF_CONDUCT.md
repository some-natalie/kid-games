# Code of Conduct

We want a friendly, welcoming community for people of all backgrounds and
experience levels. This applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and
other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people; assume good intent and accept feedback.
- Welcome beginners and different perspectives.
- Respect privacy, especially that of children and testers.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual content, personal attacks, and
publishing someone's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

The project maintainer, [@some-natalie](https://github.com/some-natalie), is
responsible for enforcement. To report a concern, use a private contact channel
the maintainer has made available on their profile. If no private channel is
available, request one without describing the incident or identifying anyone
publicly. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues.

The maintainer will review reports, protect reporter privacy as far as practical,
and may request a correction, remove content, or temporarily or permanently
restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and
its impact. Reports involving the maintainer may also be submitted through
GitHub's platform reporting mechanisms.
