Choose how to play

Quiz mode removes examples, hints, and immediate answer checks from all games. Children can change answers as often as they like before submitting, then see a percentage correct and expandable answer details to share with you.

The setting travels with links on this site using ?quiz=1, not a saved browser preference. Share a quiz-mode link to use the same setting on another device. Turn it off here to return to normal play.

Quiz answers are not saved when you leave or reload a game. Normal saved game progress is left alone. Each completely correct question earns one point; unanswered questions count as incorrect. In Acorn Adventure, later stops carry the child's entered total without revealing the correct answer; each stop is scored against the original journey.
