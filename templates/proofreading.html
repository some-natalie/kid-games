What is proofreading?

Proofreading means reading carefully to catch mistakes before you share your writing.

Tap the whole word if any of its letters look wrong. Ordinary punctuation belongs to its word; tap punctuation mistakes or wide gaps separately. Then choose Check to see how you did.

Try a quick example

Every sentence starts with a capital letter. Tap the whole word my below, then choose Check example.

Tap “my” to mark it. Tap it again to unmark it.

Here are the kinds of mistakes to hunt for:

  • Capital letters that are missing or in the wrong place
  • Commas and periods in the wrong spot
  • Spacing between words, and after punctuation
  • Spelling of tricky words
  • Flipped letters or numbers
  • Sound-alike words, like there, their, and they’re

Finish all 10 levels to complete the case! The mistakes start small, and the last level is a whole paragraph.

    How to play

    Tap the whole word if any letter looks wrong, or tap a punctuation mark or wide gap. Tap again to unmark it. Then choose Check. Fixed letters are bold and underlined. Removed marks stay red and crossed out; they still count as fixed. Correct marks stay fixed while you find the rest. Clear marks removes only marks that have not been fixed. With a keyboard, use the Left and Right arrow keys to move between items, and Space or Enter to mark one.
