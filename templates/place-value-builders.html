Make numbers with blocks

Build with unit cubes, ten-cube rods, 10×10 hundred flats, and 10×10×10 thousand cubes. Drag blocks from the tray into their matching places, and drag them back to remove them. Trade blocks between neighboring places to regroup; every exchange keeps the same total.

Worked example: trade 10 ones for 1 ten

14 ones = 1 ten and 4 ones

One ten is worth ten ones, so the number stays 14. The zero in 40 means 0 ones; the zero in 105 means 0 tens.

Level path

  1. Levels 1–3: build with ones, then exchange ten ones for a ten and a ten back into ones.
  2. Levels 4–5: compare two-digit quantities and show 40 as 4 tens and 0 ones.
  3. Levels 6–7: build 105 and exchange ten tens for one hundred.
  4. Levels 8–10: compare three-digit numbers and regroup to build 206.
  5. Levels 11–12: stack ten hundred flats into a thousand cube and build 1234.

There is no timer and no score to lose. You can also click or tap a tray block to add it, click a placed block to remove it, or use Tab and Enter. Exchange buttons provide the same trades. Ask for a hint and retry as often as you like.
