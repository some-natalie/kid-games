Make fractions with ribbons

Every ribbon is one whole. Fold marks split it into equal lengths, and you can select the parts with a tap, click, or keyboard.

Worked example: show one half

12 34

Fold the whole into four equal parts. Select two: 2/4. Those two quarters have the same length as 1/2 of the whole.

Level path

  1. Levels 1–3: fold halves and quarters; select 1/2, 1/4, and 3/4.
  2. Levels 4–5: select 2/3, then align halves and quarters to see 1/2 = 2/4.
  3. Levels 6–7: explore eighths and sixths: 1/2 = 4/8 and 1/3 = 2/6.
  4. Levels 8–9: compare 3/4 with 2/3, then complete a whole from 5/8.

There is no timer and no score to lose. Ask for a hint and try as many folds and selections as you need.
