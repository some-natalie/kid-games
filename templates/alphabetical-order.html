A quick ABC lesson

What does alphabetical order mean?

It means putting words in the same order as the letters in the alphabet. A comes before B, and B comes before C.

apple comes before banana because A comes before B.

If two words start with the same letter, look at the next letter. Keep going until you find a letter that is different!
