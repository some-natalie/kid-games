A number-line adventure

Help Tilly cross the fraction bridge

Fractions are numbers with positions. Help Tilly find where each fraction belongs between 0 and 1.

Worked example: find one half

Split the space from 0 to 1 into two equal intervals. Count one interval from 0: 1/2 is halfway across.

0 —|— 1/2 —|— 1

The denominator tells how many equal intervals make one whole. Count the spaces between tick marks, not the tick marks.

Eight untimed levels start with halves and quarters, then explore thirds, sixths, eighths, equivalent fractions, ordering, and comparing. Guides fade as you learn; a hint can bring the equal partitions back.

There is no timer. Tap a position or use Tab and Enter or Space. You can try as often as you like—there are no lost points.
